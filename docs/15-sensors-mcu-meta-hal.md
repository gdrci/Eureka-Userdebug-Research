# 15 - Sensors, MCU and Meta HALs

[中文版](15-sensors-mcu-meta-hal.zh-CN.md)

## Summary

The sensors-mcu-meta-hal group has 263 rows: 259 regular files and 4 symlinks, spread over odm_a (43), product_a (19), system_a (67), system_ext_a (123) and vendor_a (11). Its core is the vendor sensors HAL: an odm-partition HIDL service (vendor.oculus.hardware.sensors@1.0-service) built on vendor.oculus.hardware.sensors@1.0.so. It reads a TOML config at /vendor/etc/sensorservice.cfg and is selected at boot by persist.vendor.oculus.sensor.service.impl, which odm_a/etc/build.prop sets to sensorservice. Alternative implementations sit beside it: a mock HAL (disabled, no boot trigger), sensoremu and sensor-emulator, plus flicker and IAD HAL services. The framework side is the system-partition sensorservice daemon with libsensorservice.so and android.hardware.sensors 1.0/2.0/2.1 client libraries, and sensorproxy runs from system_ext. Adjacent HALs in the same classification are the sensorlock AIDL HAL, catty, dock, telemetry and devicecert, along with feature and privileged-permission XML. The group also contains TorchScript IMU-only tracker archives, PyTorch Lite microgesture archives, and the odm keylayout and keychar for oculus-device. Paths are relative to the root of the image.

The role table has one row for each of the 263 files in this group (175 verified, 13 observed, 75 inferred, 0 unverified). It is at `data/subsystems/sensors-mcu-meta-hal/roles.tsv`.

## Verified claims

- **C01** (verified): All 263 input rows appear exactly once in roles.tsv (header plus 263 data rows), with identical partition and path keys. Confidence counts: verified 175, observed 13, inferred 75.
- **C02** (verified): Recomputed sha256 digests of all 259 regular-file rows match the inventory digests. No two regular-file rows share a digest.
- **C03** (verified): Group composition: 43 odm_a, 19 product_a, 67 system_a, 123 system_ext_a, 11 vendor_a rows. Kinds: 259 file and 4 symlink. The symlinks are two ART vdex links to ../boot-com.oculus.os.platform.vdex (system_a arm64 and arm), system_a/system/bin/touch to toybox, and vendor_a/bin/touch to toybox_vendor.
- **C04** (verified): Several same-size library pairs differ in content across partitions: odm_a and system_ext_a copies of lib64/vendor.oculus.hardware.sensors@1.0.so are both 1534496 bytes with different digests; telemetry@1.0.so is 87296 bytes in odm_a, product_a and system_ext_a with three different digests.
- **C05** (verified): The sensors HAL service is a stripped aarch64 PIE with interpreter /system/bin/linker64. Its NEEDED list includes libsensorutils.so, libvrsensors-hidlwrapper.so, android.hardware.sensors@1.0.so and @2.0.so, vendor.oculus.hardware.sensors@1.0.so, vendor.oculus.hardware.sensors_java@1.0.so, vendor.oculus.hardware.dock@1.0.so, libsyncboss.so, libcamerahal.so, libsensortelemetry.so, libdevicesensors.so and libtrackingvendorutils.so. All NEEDED libraries checked are present in the mounted partitions.
- **C06** (verified): The sensors HAL binary reads /vendor/etc/sensorservice.cfg and parses TOML keys default_rate and fallback. Log messages cover IMU and mag rate parsing and fallback to the TOML default rate.
- **C07** (verified): The sensorservice.cfg file is TOML with top-level nice_constellation = "ruby", [imu.default_rate] fallback = 800 and [mag.default_rate] fallback = 25. Comments say the device-specific keys must match kDeviceVersionName in libdeviceid. The same nice_constellation line appears in mrsystemservice.cfg and trackingservice.cfg.
- **C09** (verified): The mock sensors HAL service vendor.oculus.sensors-mock-hal-1-0 is declared disabled and has no on-boot trigger in its rc file. Its onrestart lines restart calibration_svr, mrsystemservice, sensorproxy and trackingservice.
- **C10** (verified): The VINTF manifest for the odm sensors HAL declares 10 interfaces (ICameraProvider, IControllerProvider, IExternalTimingProvider, IFlickerSensor, IIad, IImu, IMag, ISensorsMiscDebug, IStructuredLightProjector, IZapper). The HIDL library carries 40 distinct vendor.oculus.hardware.sensors@1.0::<name> strings. The SELinux hwservice contexts list 19 sensors-related entries, including IAccel, ICamera, ICameraStream, IGyro, IEventHaptics and IPowerstate. The lists do not match, and runtime registration is not determined.
- **C11** (verified): The vendor sensors HIDL library exports the HIDL interface symbols for about 25 named interfaces, including ICameraStream, IControllerStateProvider, IEventHaptics, IFaceEyeTrackingLed, IMotionSensorProvider and ISensorClient, beyond the manifest set. Its SONAME is vendor.oculus.hardware.sensors@1.0.so.
- **C12** (verified): The sensor emulator is provided by two init services. Service vendor.sensoremu (/odm/bin/sensoremu) runs as root and is oneshot and disabled. Service vendor.sensor-emulator (/odm/bin/sensor-emulator) is the same shape. Both have on-boot triggers gated on the sensor impl property. SELinux maps both binaries to hal_oculus_sensors_default_exec, and their data directories /data/vendor/misc/sensoremu and /data/vendor/misc/sensor-emulator to sensoremu_odm_file and sensor-emulator_odm_file.
- **C13** (verified): The sensorlock HAL is an AIDL service registered as vendor.oculus.hardware.sensorlock.ISensorLock/default. It is paired with an AIDL NDK stub library (ISensorLock, ISensorLockListener), a libsensorlock.so backend that links libsmcihandler.so, and a /dev/sensorlock node. The node is labelled sensorlock_device and its ueventd mode is 0444 system system.
- **C14** (verified): The IAD and flicker sensor HAL services are separate binaries. IAD is service vendor.oculus.iad-hal-1-0 in class early_hal, providing IIad, with task_profiles trackingPolicy. Flicker is service vendor.oculus.flicker-hal-1-0 in class hal, providing IFlickerSensor. The flicker backend libflickersensor.so links libstals.so and libtelemetry.so. SELinux labels the flicker and IAD binaries and maps the flicker device nodes /dev/vd6281 and /dev/vd6281_spi.
- **C15** (verified): The catty, dock and telemetry HAL services are separate binaries. Catty (ICatty, ICattyPipeClient) runs as service vendor.catty-1-0. Dock (IDock, IDockstateCallback) runs as service vendor.dock-1-0. Telemetry (IDeviceTelemetry) runs from system_ext as oculus-hardware-telemetry-1-0. The compatibility matrix lists catty and dock as optional HIDL HALs, and the hwservice contexts map them to dedicated hwservice types.
- **C16** (verified): sensorproxy is a system_ext service (/system_ext/bin/sensorproxy, class late_start, user system, disabled). It is started on boot only when persist.ovr.enable.sensorproxy=true and stopped when it is false. Its binary links libsensorclientutils, libsensordatatransforms, libcameramuxmodemonitor and libfaceeyetrackingledprovider. Its file context is sensorproxy_exec.
- **C17** (verified): The system sensorservice daemon creates SensorService, starts the binder thread pool and registers with the default service manager. It links libsensorservice.so, libsensorprivacy.so and libbinder.so. Its service name sensorservice maps to sensorservice_service and android.hardware.sensors::ISensors maps to hal_sensors_hwservice.
- **C18** (verified): libsensorservice.so (lib64) exports the SensorService constructor and addProximityActiveListener and removeProximityActiveListener methods. The library reads the debug.sensors property, and its log strings include Sensors HAL died, attempting to reconnect.
- **C20** (verified): sensor_tool is a CLI for sensor and camera stream debugging. It parses TOML and JSON configuration (policy values Realtime, Normal and Batch), dumps frames to a directory selected with --out, and links libEGL, libGLESv3, libgui and libvrsensors-hidlwrapper. sensor_bench is an FMQ-based benchmark CLI that reads and writes CPU frequencies.
- **C21** (observed): sensor_validation is invoked by the system svt.sh script with per-device configuration names of the form SensorValidation_<device>_Stu, for example SensorValidation_Eureka_Stu. The standalone variant links vendor.oculus.hardware.sensors@1.0.so and libsensorclientutils.so.
- **C22** (verified): Sensor property and config keys referenced by group ELFs are: persist.vendor.ovr.imu.direct_channel (libvrsensors-hidlwrapper.so in odm_a and system_a), persist.vendor.oculus.sensor.service.enable_high_frequency_telemetry and persist.vendor.oculus.sensor.service.disable_telemetry_submission (libsensortelemetry.so in odm_a and system_a), and debug.sensors (libsensorservice.so in system_a).
- **C24** (verified): The handtracking microgesture files are PyTorch Lite zip archives with archive/ prefixes, under the paths nimble_gesture/microgesture/model. MGV23, MGV23MG, MGV24MG and MGV25MG are the four variants. The tracking engine library contains the strings MGV23, MGV23MG and MGV24MG. No MGV25MG string was found in the scanned binaries. - **C25** (verified): The oculus-device key layout maps scancode 172 to HOME and 159 to FORWARD. The key character map sets FORWARD with base fallback HOME. The odm IDC file Vendor_2833_Product_0160.idc (outside this group) binds keyboard.layout and keyboard.characterMap to oculus-device.

## Gaps

- No disassembly or decompilation was performed. HIDL and AIDL method signatures, call graphs and runtime behaviour were not extracted; the only interface facts are descriptor strings and exported symbols.
- All ELF binaries are stripped or lightly symbolised, so their roles beyond the imports and strings listed in the evidence are name-based (75 rows inferred).
- Which HAL instance a shipping device runs is decided by a runtime property. Only odm_a/etc/build.prop was checked for the default (sensorservice). Other property sources such as persist data, /data overrides or vendor_a/build.prop were not searched for this key.
- The interface lists disagree: the odm manifest declares 10 interfaces, the library has 40 vendor.oculus.hardware.sensors@1.0:: strings, and the SELinux hwservice contexts list 19 sensors entries. Which interfaces are registered at runtime was not determined.
- SELinux policy was read only through grep of text .cil and *_contexts files. The precompiled_sepolicy binary under odm_a/etc/selinux and any policy binaries were not decoded, so the full allow set was not enumerated.
- The 32-bit variants under system_a/lib and vendor_a/lib, and the 32-bit odm and product copies, were not inspected beyond their names and sizes.
- meta.bin under system_ext_a/etc/bootanim (1081352 bytes) was not identified; its format remains unknown.
- The TorchScript and PyTorch Lite archives were not decoded. Tensor shapes, input and output names, and the versioning semantics were not extracted. Producer metadata contains build host and user identifiers, which were deliberately not reproduced.
- The exact TOML grammar and the device-version names used by sensorservice.cfg (kDeviceVersionName in libdeviceid) were not extracted, so the key-matching behaviour is only described from strings and comments.
- sensor_validation (8.2 MB) and libsensorvalidation.so were classified from name, NEEDED list and the svt.sh invocation only; their internal test cases were not examined.
- The four symlinks point to toybox and to boot vdex files outside this group; those targets were not analysed.
- Libraries listed as dependencies of the sensors HAL and emulator but outside this group's rows (libsyncboss.so, libcamerahal.so, libclocksync.so, libwavreader.so, libgammacorrection.so, libcalibrationstore.so) were confirmed present but not inspected.
