# 10 - Display and composer

[中文版](10-display-and-composer.zh-CN.md)

## Summary

The group holds 132 rows (odm_a 6, system_a 65, system_ext_a 7, vendor_a 54; 130 regular files and 2 symlinks) that make up the Android display, graphics-composition and DRM stack of a Qualcomm SDM/SDE-based device with Oculus VR (VROS) components. Two IComposer 2.4 HAL implementations are registered in VINTF: the Qualcomm vendor.qti.hardware.display.composer-service (IQtiComposer 3.2, DisplayConfig AIDL) and the Oculus StrataComposer service (instance "display", disabled at boot and enabled only when a persist.device_config property is true). surfaceflinger is the HIDL client of both, and gralloc, mapper, allocator and DisplayConfig client libraries sit beneath them. Display tuning comes from /system/etc/display.conf (JSON), /vendor/etc/display/DPU930.xml (Qualcomm SDE930 parameter table, read by libdisplayqos.so) and the vendor property vendor.display.*. About 70 rows are DRM and media-DRM material (ClearKey and Widevine HAL services, DRM framework, MediaDrm metrics, forward-lock engine, memtrack). libdrm.so here is the userspace kernel-DRM library, not Android content-protection DRM.

The role table has one row for each of the 132 files in this group (114 verified, 13 observed, 5 inferred, 0 unverified). It is at `data/subsystems/display-and-composer/roles.tsv`.

## Verified claims

- **C02** (verified): roles.tsv has a header and 132 data rows, one per input row, with keys identical to the input. Confidence split: 114 verified, 13 observed, 5 inferred, 0 unverified.
- **C03** (verified): The Qualcomm composer service is an ELF64 PIE for AArch64, dynamically linked against /system/bin/linker64, stripped, with 41 NEEDED entries.
- **C04** (verified): The Qualcomm composer service links the composer HIDL 2.1 to 2.4 and vendor QTI 3.0 to 3.2 interfaces, plus libsdmcore.so, libqservice.so, libdisplayconfig.qti.so, libdrm.so and libdisplaydebug.so. Those last three are in this group.
- **C05** (verified): The Qualcomm composer service registers IQtiComposer 3.2 (instance default), IComposer 2.4 (instance default), and the DisplayConfig AIDL (vendor.qti.hardware.display.config version 10, fqname IDisplayConfig/default). The manifest also lists vendor.display.config IDisplayConfig 2.0.
- **C06** (verified): The Qualcomm composer service's init definition (service name vendor.qti.hardware.display.composer) is in a standalone rc file and also in init.anorak.rc, where the copy is marked override and disabled, restarts the strata class on restart, and is started when vendor.syncbosshal.fw_update_complete=true.
- **C07** (verified): SELinux labels the Qualcomm composer service as hal_graphics_composer_default_exec and registers IQtiComposer on the hwservice label hal_graphics_composer_hwservice.
- **C10** (verified): The Oculus StrataComposer service is an ELF64 PIE for AArch64 with 30 NEEDED entries. These include composer HIDL 2.1 to 2.4, mapper 2.0, 3.0 and 4.0, allocator 4.0, power 1.3, libEGL.so, libGLESv2.so and vendor.qti.hardware.display.mapper@4.0.so.
- **C11** (verified): The Oculus composer advertises IComposer 2.4 with instance name display in VINTF, marked override=true. Its binary contains the strings 'Starting VROS HWComposer HAL' and 'StrataComposerService_2_4'.
- **C12** (verified): The Oculus composer service is disabled in init and is enabled only when persist.device_config.oculus_shared_os_services.mros_enable_strata_composer is true. The property defaults to false. When enabled, init sets debug.sf.hwc_service_name and ro.ovr.hwc_service_name to display.
- **C14** (verified): SELinux labels the Oculus composer service as strata_exec, and a type transition gives it the strata domain when init executes it.
- **C15** (verified): surfaceflinger is an ELF64 PIE for AArch64 with 43 NEEDED entries, including composer HIDL 2.1 to 2.4, allocator 2.0 and 3.0, android.frameworks.displayservice@1.0.so and libdisplayservicehidl.so.
- **C17** (observed): surfaceflinger reads debug.sf.* properties (for example debug.sf.hwc_service_name, debug.sf.enable_layer_caching, debug.sf.disable_writeback_display) and a sysprop has_writeback_dpu_cac from libSurfaceFlingerProp.so.
- **C20** (verified): display.conf is a JSON object with 10 keys: DIRECTION 3, SHUTTER_TYPE 0, two COMMENT-valued keys, SWAP_TIMING_FRONT_BUFFER and SWAP_TIMING_SWAPPED_BUFFER (6-element arrays), NIGHT_DISPLAY_AVAILABLE true, and night-display colour temperature minimum 2220, default 2856, maximum 6504.
- **C23** (verified): The system copy of the composer 2.4 library exports the V2_4 IComposerClient and IComposerCallback proxy and cast functions. The HIDL interface name is android.hardware.graphics.composer::IComposer on the label hal_graphics_composer_hwservice. The vendor copy is a different file with 345 defined dynamic symbols.
- **C24** (verified): The 64-bit mapper 4.0 QTI implementation exports HIDL_FETCH_IMapper and HIDL_FETCH_IQtiMapper plus QtiMapperExtensions methods. It NEEDs libgralloccore.so, libgrallocutils.so, libqdMetaData.so, vendor QTI mapper 4.0 and mapperextensions 1.0 to 1.2. It is labelled same_process_hal_file.
- **C25** (verified): The 64-bit gralloc module exports the HMI module-info symbol and gralloc_lock, gralloc_unlock, gralloc_register_buffer, gralloc_unregister_buffer, mapBuffer, terminateBuffer, mapFrameBufferLocked and fb_device_open. It has 6 NEEDED entries and is labelled same_process_hal_file.
- **C26** (verified): The 64-bit DisplayConfig client library exports DisplayConfig::ClientImpl methods such as SetActiveConfig, SetPowerMode, IsHDRSupported and SetCWBOutputBuffer. It has 150 defined dynamic symbols and NEEDs vendor.display.config@2.0.so. It is labelled same_process_hal_file.
- **C28** (observed): display_tool addresses these kernel and sysfs nodes: /sys/class/backlight/, /sys/class/drm/card0-sde-crtc-0/, /sys/class/drm/card0/card0-sde-crtc-0/backlight_temperature_override, and /sys/kernel/debug/dri/0/DSI-1/dcs_cmd_tx and dcs_cmd_tx_unicast.
- **C29** (verified): display_property_handler is a one-shot helper. Init starts it with the display.enable_ddic_cac property as its argument, once vendor.skip.init=0 and display.enable_ddic_cac is set. It opens the DRM render node and issues DRM_IOCTL_MSM_DISPLAY_CAC through libdrm. Its SELinux label is disp_prop_handler_exec.
- **C30** (verified): The ClearKey DRM HAL service is an ELF64 PIE with 17 NEEDED entries. It registers clearkey DRM and crypto factories for interfaces 1.0 to 1.4. It is lazy and disabled until init starts it, runs as user media, and is labelled vendor_hal_drm_clearkey_exec. Its service name is vendor.drm-clearkey-hal-1-4.
- **C31** (verified): drmserver is an ELF32 PIE for ARM. It is the Android DRM framework daemon, started only when drm.service.enabled is set to true or 1. It runs as user drm. It NEEDs libdrmframeworkcommon.so, libbinder.so, libselinux.so and libstagefright_foundation.so, and is labelled drmserver_exec. Its service-manager name is drm.drmManager.
- **C32** (verified): libdisplayservicehidl.so implements the android.frameworks.displayservice@1.0 HIDL service: DisplayService::getEventReceiver and DisplayEventReceiver with requestNextVsync, setVsyncRate and init. It is labelled fwk_display_hwservice and listed in the framework manifest.
- **C33** (verified): The odm anorak Oculus composer 1.1 implementation exports HIDL_FETCH_IComposer. It NEEDs libdisplayconfig.qti.so and libdrm.so, and its VINTF fragment declares IComposer 1.1 with transport passthrough and instance default.
- **C34** (observed): Two IComposer 2.4 HAL services are declared in VINTF, with different instance names: the Qualcomm service uses instance default and the Oculus service uses instance display. Their sysprop-selected service name is set to display only when the Oculus service is enabled.
- **C35** (observed): In the mounted partitions, the libdrm.so files are the userspace libdrm library: they export drmGetDevice2, drmAddBufs and related functions. They are distinct from the Android DRM framework libraries (libdrmframework, libmediadrm) and the HAL libraries.
- **C36** (verified): The mediadrm signer is a jar (classes.dex and MANIFEST.MF only) registered as a shared library by a permissions XML. Its arm and arm64 vdex files are byte-identical.
- **C37** (verified): The two hwclock rows are symbolic links: system_a/system/bin/hwclock points to toybox and vendor_a/bin/hwclock to toybox_vendor.
- **C38** (verified): The Widevine and ClearKey HAL service binaries are lazy, disabled until started, and use the same interface numbering. The Widevine service registers the widevine DRM and crypto factories over interfaces 1.0 to 1.4.

## Claims with corrected wording

These claims held in substance. The wording below replaces the original.

- **C01** (verified): The group has 132 input rows: 130 regular files and 2 symlinks, across odm_a (6), system_a (65), system_ext_a (7) and vendor_a (54). All four partitions are mounted read-only. Every regular file exists under the mount and matches its recorded size and sha256. The 2 symlink rows (system_a/system/bin/hwclock -> toybox, vendor_a/bin/hwclock -> toybox_vendor) record no sha256 (field '-') and are checked as links only.
- **C08** (observed): The Qualcomm composer service binary contains 41 strings beginning vendor.display., vendor.gralloc. or vendor.debug.; 39 of them are concrete property keys (the other two are the bare prefix vendor.display. and the template vendor.display.cac_ipd_adjust_%zu). Examples: vendor.display.enable_verbose_log, vendor.display.cac_scale_red, vendor.display.idle_time, vendor.display.disable_skip_validate, vendor.gralloc.disable_ubwc.
- **C09** (observed): The Qualcomm composer service binary contains the path strings /vendor/etc/display/hwc_display_resolution_extn.xml, /data/vendor/display and /data/vendor/display/frame_dump_external, and the dump filename formats %s/input_layer%d_%dx%d_%s_frame%d.raw and %s/frame_dump_disp_id_%02u_%s. The resolution XML is not present in the mounted vendor_a/etc/display/ (which holds only DPU930.xml). The roles of these paths (extension resolution list, frame dumps) are inferred from their names.
- **C13** (verified): The Oculus composer service rc sets ro.surface_flinger.has_writeback_dpu_cac to 0 (line 29) when the strata composer property is true and ro.vros.composer.cac_mode is writeback, and sets it to 1 (line 34) whenever ro.vros.composer.cac_mode is writeback, with no condition on the strata property. When strata is enabled both actions match and init runs them in file order, so the value ends as 1 (inferred from init's in-order execution); the rc comment 'unless set by Strata above' is not enforced. The service binary reads ro.vros.composer.cac_mode and ro.vros.composer.orientation.
- **C16** (verified): surfaceflinger is defined in system_a/system/etc/init/surfaceflinger.rc as /system/bin/surfaceflinger in the init classes core and animation (class core animation), with SYS_NICE and three PDX sockets (pdx/system/vr/display/client, manager, vsync). Its SELinux label is surfaceflinger_exec. The vendor init.anorak.rc also defines the service, marked override and disabled, and starts it from the vendor.syncbosshal.fw_update_complete=true action; the text does not settle which definition init applies.
- **C18** (verified): DPU930.xml is an XML document, 2108 bytes, sha256 e46c07687f208cc0b715d4ae1ff8c79e6873d15e9ed4b715e35683384e73c4c6. Its root element is <XML>, whose only child is the SDE930 element. SDE930 contains 74 active Param entries (Param0 to Param73) and a commented Overrides example at lines 83 to 89. The meanings of the numeric parameters are not determined.
- **C19** (observed): libdisplayqos.so (64-bit and 32-bit copies) has a string table containing /vendor/etc/display/ and the DPU model file names DPU660.xml, DPU670.xml, DPU6__.xml, DPU720.xml, DPU7__.xml, DPU820.xml, DPU830.xml, DPU8__.xml and DPU930.xml. It is the only mounted binary that contains the name DPU930.xml, so it is the likely reader of the DPU parameter files (inferred; the code that reads them was not traced).
- **C21** (verified): The only mounted file that references /system/etc/display.conf is vrdevicemanagerserver in system_ext_a, which has no group row (outside this group). No init rc or SELinux file in the mounted partitions refers to display.conf. A plain search for display\\.conf also matches the display.config service-context names, so the check must be word-bounded.
- **C22** (verified): The vendor QTI composer 3.2 library (vendor_a/lib64) exports the IQtiComposer and IQtiComposerClient classes (castFrom and related functions), has 269 defined dynamic symbols, and NEEDs the graphics common 1.0 to 1.2 libraries, composer 2.1 to 2.4, and vendor QTI composer 3.0 and 3.1 libraries.
- **C27** (observed): display_tool is an ELF64 PIE for AArch64 that is stripped and uses linker64. It prints usage for many subcommands, including brightness, configure_cabc, configure_cabc_gray_interp, configure_cac, lock_backlight, overide_display_temperature, read_dcs, write_dcs, write_dcs_unicast, toggle_cabc, toggle_cabc_gray_interp, toggle_cac, toggle_display_power and refresh_rate. It prints 'Root permissions required. Run adb root and try again.' It NEEDs vendor.oculus.hardware.graphics.composer@1.1.so. No other file in the mounted partitions contains the string display_tool.

## Gaps

- Binaries are stripped. Behaviour is taken from dynamic exports, strings, NEEDED lists and init/VINTF/SELinux text. No disassembly or decompilation was performed, so internal call graphs and parameter semantics are unknown.
- Runtime selection between the Qualcomm composer service and the Oculus StrataComposer is not determined. It depends on the persist.device_config.oculus_shared_os_services.mros_enable_strata_composer value on the device, which is not in the image, and on init ordering.
- Two definitions of the service vendor.qti.hardware.display.composer exist (standalone rc and init.anorak.rc with override and disabled). The effective definition under init is not determined from the text.
- Meanings of the 74 numeric Param entries in DPU930.xml and of the display.conf keys beyond their names and values are not determined. libdisplayqos.so's parsing logic was not examined.
- display.conf has no consumer inside this group. The only consumer identified is vrdevicemanagerserver in system_ext_a, outside this group.
- No SELinux label for system_ext_a/bin/display_tool or display_validator was found in the text policy and file_contexts files. The compiled precompiled_sepolicy binary was not parsed.
- Exported-symbol enumeration was not performed for several 32-bit libraries: libhdr_tm.so, libsdedrm.so, libwvdrmengine.so, libdrmfs.so, libdrmtime.so and libmemtrackproxy.so. Their roles are taken from NEEDED lists and names; libdrmfs, libdrmtime and both libmemtrackproxy rows are labelled inferred.
- Dependencies outside this group were not classified and are only named: libsdmcore.so, libqservice.so, libqdutils.so, libqdMetaData.so, libgralloccore.so, libgrallocutils.so, libgralloctypes.so, vendor.qti.hardware.display.mapper@4.0.so, vendor.display.config@2.0.so, android.hardware.graphics.common@1.x, android.hardware.power@1.3.so, libhardware.so, libprotobuf-cpp-lite-3.9.1.so and libQSEEComAPI.so.
- Expansions of the adaptive-backlight acronyms CABL, FOSS and SVI in libdisp-aba are not confirmed. Only the symbol prefixes were observed.
- Whether the 32-bit or 64-bit anorak composer implementation is the one loaded on a running device is not determined.
- The Widevine engine (libwvdrmengine.so) internals and the content-protection behaviour of the DRM stack were not analysed. Security properties are out of scope.
- The contents described here are the mounted copies of the partitions, not the raw super image.
- Roles for the five inferred rows (libmemtrackproxy.so in both variants, libdrmfs.so and libdrmtime.so in 32-bit and 64-bit variants) rest on NEEDED lists and file names only.
- The value of display.vendor.skip.init and display.enable_ddic_cac on a running device is not determined, so whether display_property_handler runs is unknown.
